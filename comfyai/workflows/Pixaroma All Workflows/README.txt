PIXAROMA ALL WORKFLOWS

Every workflow from the Pixaroma ComfyUI course, one folder per episode.
43 episodes, made 2026-11-17. A fresh copy is made every time a new episode is published:
https://workflows.pixaroma.com/

NOT INCLUDED: the Media Inputs (the example pictures, audio, video and 3D models used in the
videos). They are in each episode's own zip. The episodes that have them are marked below:
open that episode's page and download its zip.

HOW TO ADD THEM TO COMFYUI
Copy this whole "Pixaroma All Workflows" folder into your workflows folder:
  Easy-Install: ComfyUI-Easy-Install/ComfyUI/user/default/workflows
  Portable:     ComfyUI_windows_portable/ComfyUI/user/default/workflows
  (no "workflows" folder there yet? make one)
Then in ComfyUI open the Workflows panel on the left and press its refresh button.
Quick option: drag a .json file straight onto the ComfyUI canvas.
1 minute video: https://workflows.pixaroma.com/help/open-workflow/
Missing nodes or models? Watch that episode's video. Help: https://workflows.pixaroma.com/help/
